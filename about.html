A note from Wajid

Welcome to my unfinished story.

I am not writing this because I have figured life out. I am writing because, after nearly four decades of living it, I have come to appreciate how much there still is to understand.

My life has taken me from working as a teenage cashier in Manhattan to building a career in technology, and from the familiarity of New York to distant corners of the world.

This is a collection of moments that have made me question what it means to live well. It is not a guide to living a perfect life, nor an attempt to tell anyone how they should live.

Perhaps somewhere between my experiences and your own, we will discover that we are not as different as we sometimes imagine.

And perhaps, together, we can leave behind a little more light than we found.

Wajid
